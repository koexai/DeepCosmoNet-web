Provenance and Data Generation Methods
This dataset collection originates from cosmological simulations and subsequent structure identification analysis, developed within two distinct academic and commercial research contexts.

1. N-body Simulation Particle Data (sample.csv)

Acquisition: The particle data was generated from a DEMNUni type N-body cosmological simulation. The simulation box has a side length of 1 Gpc. This work was conducted by Prof. Carmelita Carbone at the University of Milan in 2024. The data represents a sample of the full simulation, containing positional coordinates (x, y, z), velocity components (velx, vely, velz), a unique particle identifier (iord), and the unitary mass.

2. Subhalo and Void Catalogs (catalog_sample.csv, sample_voids.csv)

Processing & Derivations: The catalogs of subhalos and cosmic voids were generated by Koexai Srl in 2025 as part of the "DeepCosmoNet" research project. These structures were identified by applying structure-finding algorithms to the N-body simulation data.

The subhalo catalog (catalog_sample.csv) contains the properties of gravitationally bound structures, including the number of member particles, total mass, and the centroid's position.

The void catalog (sample_voids.csv) is complementary to the subhalo catalog and identifies the large, under-dense regions of the cosmic web. For each void, the position of its center and its effective radius are provided.
